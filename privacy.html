Privacy Policy

Wonder Shelf Automation · Effective date: 2026-11-02

This Privacy Policy explains how Wonder Shelf Automation (the "App") accesses, uses, stores and shares information. The App is an internal tool operated by Wonder Shelf to manage the YouTube channel Wonder Shelf (@thewondershelves). It is not offered to the public and does not serve other users' data.

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. This policy also incorporates the Google Privacy Policy.

1. Information the App accesses

After you sign in with your Google account and approve the requested permissions, the App accesses the following data, and only for the Wonder Shelf channel:

  • Channel details: channel ID, name, description, statistics and video list.
  • Video data: titles, descriptions, tags, privacy status, thumbnails, and the video files you choose to upload.
  • Playlists: lists and playlist items of the channel.
  • Comments: comment threads on the channel's own videos, and replies you post.
  • Analytics and reporting: views, watch time, retention and similar channel reports from the YouTube Analytics and YouTube Reporting APIs.
  • Account authorization: an OAuth access token and refresh token that Google issues to the App.

2. How the information is used

  • Uploading videos, setting thumbnails and updating video metadata, but only after the channel owner has reviewed and approved each upload.
  • Managing playlists, and reading or replying to comments on the channel's own videos.
  • Producing channel performance summaries for the channel owner.

The App does not use YouTube data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to managing the Wonder Shelf channel.

3. Storage

The App runs on the channel owner's own computer. OAuth tokens are stored only in a local file on that computer. Retrieved YouTube data is kept only locally, as needed to operate the App. The App does not send YouTube data or tokens to any server operated by the developer.

When the channel owner uses an AI assistant (Claude Code, by Anthropic) to operate the App, data returned by the YouTube APIs can appear in that assistant session and is processed by Anthropic under its own terms.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share YouTube user data with third parties. Data is exchanged only with Google's APIs, as needed to perform the functions above. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Revoking access

You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. You can also delete the App's local token file, which signs the App out on that computer.

6. Deletion

  • If you revoke access in the App, all authorized data accessed or stored under that consent is deleted within 7 calendar days.
  • If you revoke access through Google's security settings page, all YouTube API data related to you is deleted within 30 calendar days.
  • To request deletion of stored data at any time, open an issue at https://github.com/MuhammadIbrahim2555/wondershelf/issues. Requests are completed as soon as possible and within 7 calendar days.

7. Your choices and contact

To ask a question, raise a complaint, or exercise any right described here, contact Wonder Shelf: open an issue at https://github.com/MuhammadIbrahim2555/wondershelf/issues.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The effective date at the top of the page shows the latest version.
